Rebuild from repository root:

horse.glb: +Z forward, +Y up, feet at Y=0. Hip height approximately 1.6m.
Approximate rider seat: [0, 1.67, 0.42] in model space.
Idle: 3 seconds. Walk: 1.2 seconds. Run: 0.666667 seconds (running trot).
14,986 triangles, 19 joints, 5 mesh primitives, one skeleton.

Native renderer compatibility: all five primitives share one opaque material and
one embedded 3072x2048 albedo atlas, also saved as textures/horse_albedo.png.
Coat retains 2048x2048 source resolution; hair occupies a 1024x1024 region and
eyes a 512x512 region. The atlas combines color data without baking lights.
Original alpha hair cards use dark opaque backing for the current built-in
skinned material. No normal texture is needed by the final GLB.
