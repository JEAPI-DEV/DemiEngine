Official API files records and MD5 source integrity hashes: downloads.json.
Downloaded 2026-10-18. Meshes reduced, separated by material and exported as
reusable source GLBs; original texture coordinates preserved. Original 2K maps
retained. Autumn leaves derive source color into ochre/gold, combining the
separate official leaf opacity map into real RGBA PNG. Sky derives 2K HDRI
into an AgX tone-mapped 2K PNG suitable for the native image loader.

Rebuild from repository root:
blender -b --python tools/demo-art/amber_pass/environment_assets.py

Missing original source files are downloaded and verified into
/tmp/demi-environment; large original source binaries are not repository files.
Editable .blend files contain production-resolution meshes, not million-triangle
source meshes. Per-model triangle counts, bounds and material roles: summary.json.
GLBs contain geometry only; assign explicit texture material assets by role.

Additional rider surface textures (official 1K CC0 PNG sources):
Fabric Pattern 07, Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07
Fabric Leather 01, Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_leather_01
Metal Plate 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02
Rider neutral albedos derive restrained grayscale variation from the original
color maps, retaining rider vertex colors as the visible cloth/leather/armor
palette. Original source maps are preserved. The metal source is worn steel,
not a promise of a brushed anisotropic shader.

ARM maps split red into AO and green into roughness with Non-Color image data.
PNG scalar outputs preserve source channel bytes exactly (verified for all four
packed maps). Rebuild maps without processing meshes:
blender -b --python tools/demo-art/amber_pass/environment_assets.py -- --materials-only

Foliage revision: original connected tree leaf surfaces become complete textured
cards with preserved source UV extents; 7,000 spatially distributed cards at
full detail, 2,000 at medium detail. Cards expand 1.85x around their own centers
to retain canopy coverage. No individual leaf triangles are discarded.
Final complete-card canopy is also in autumn_canopy.blend and tree_small_02.blend.

Photo grass: https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01 (CC0, Poly Haven).
Official authored small_a, tall_a and tiny_c patches form 833/290/40 triangle
LODs with matching metre bounds and feet at zero. Original photo colors are
gently yellowed and combined with the official 1K opacity map into real RGBA.
Native-compatible UV0 is the sole exported UV set for canopy and grass.
Rebuild foliage: blender -b --python tools/demo-art/amber_pass/foliage_assets.py
This runs automatically at the end of the complete environment rebuild.

Grass cluster construction: two tuft
centers, two leaning cutout cards each, staggered heights and tighter photo
rectangles. Original photograph colors receive reproducible linear RGB
exposure multipliers [3.15, 3.0, 2.55]; alpha remains the official opacity map.
This bakes no lighting. Current LOD triangles are recorded in summary.json.
